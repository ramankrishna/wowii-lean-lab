# wowii-lean-lab

Attempts on the open graph conjectures from *Written on the Wall II* (WOWII, the conjectures
generated by DeLaViña's Graffiti.pc), using the Lean 4 statements in
[google-deepmind/formal-conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures).
An LLM writes proofs, Lean checks them, and exhaustive search over small graphs filters out
false lemmas before anyone tries to prove them.

## Results

| Conjecture | Result |
|---|---|
| **WOWII 96**: α(G) ≤ 1 + min distEven(v) · (max λ(v) − 1) | **Proved in Lean**, standard axioms only. Not new: [PR #7665](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/pull/7665) (opened 2026-10-28) proved it first. Ours is an independent rediscovery. |
| WOWII 84: tree(G) ≥ 2·rad(G)/δ(G) | Partial. The δ ≥ 2 case is proved in Lean. The δ = 1 case is reduced to "critical" graphs (below) and is still open. |
| WOWII 72 | Partial. The case max λ ≤ 3 is proved in Lean. |

Partial results are reported but not counted. Targets and success criteria were fixed before any attempt.

**Counterexample search: 0 violations for all 8 open conjectures** across
- all 273,192 connected graphs on up to 9 vertices
- 32,181 graphs on 10 vertices
- 62,707 random and structured graphs on 10 to 14 vertices
- structured C4-free families up to 381 vertices (for WOWII 133)

The graph invariants were implemented twice, independently, to match the Lean definitions exactly,
including edge conventions such as natural-number subtraction and 0^0 = 1. Both implementations agree.

## The verifier

A model rewarded for "the file compiles" will eventually find ways to make files compile without
proving anything. A candidate proof passes only if all five checks hold:

1. **Static:** no `sorry`, `admit`, `axiom`, `unsafe`, `extern`, `implemented_by`, or kernel-check skips.
2. **Statement lock:** the theorem statement and everything before it match the original exactly.
   Helper lemmas may be added, but not definitions, instances, notation or options, since those can
   change what the statement means without changing its text.
3. **Compile:** Lean accepts the file.
4. **Type lock:** the statement's elaborated type, as Lean itself prints it, matches the original.
5. **Axioms:** only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. This rules out `native_decide` and added axioms.

Candidate files that could run code during compilation (`#eval`, `IO`, `run_cmd` and similar) are
rejected before Lean sees them.

### Calibration

The verifier is calibrated on 11 control cases on real Lean: proofs already in the repo that we didn't write, our WOWII 96 proof, and eight tampered proofs built to cheat. All 11 gave the expected verdict.

## The math

### WOWII 96 (proved)

> For connected G on at least 2 vertices: α(G) ≤ 1 + m·(L − 1), where m is the minimum over v of the
> number of vertices at even distance from v, and L is the maximum over v of α(G[N(v)]).

Choose v achieving m, let E be the vertices at even distance from v, and let S be a maximum independent set.
Send each s ∈ S to itself if s ∈ E, and otherwise to a BFS parent of s, which is one layer closer to v and so lies in E.

- At most L elements of S are sent to v: either just v itself, or an independent subset of N(v).
- At most L − 1 are sent to any other u ∈ E. If u ∈ S, only u is sent there, and L ≥ 2 because u and its
  BFS grandparent are non-adjacent neighbours of u's parent. If u ∉ S, the elements sent to u form an
  independent set inside N(u), one layer further out, and u's own parent (one layer closer in) can be added
  to that set without breaking independence.

Adding up: α ≤ L + (m − 1)(L − 1) = 1 + m(L − 1). ∎

### WOWII 84: tree(G) ≥ 2·rad(G)/δ(G)

**δ ≥ 2 (proved in Lean).** A shortest path of length rad(G) is an induced path, so tree(G) ≥ rad + 1 > 2·rad/δ.

**δ = 1 (open).** It needs an induced tree on at least 2r vertices whenever G has a vertex of degree 1.

- Erdős, Saks and Sós (1986, proof due to F. Chung) showed every connected graph of radius r has an
  induced path on 2r − 1 vertices. The δ = 1 case needs one more vertex.
- **The induced-path version is false.** A 4-cycle with a pendant vertex on each corner has radius 3 and
  longest induced path 5, but it does have an induced tree on 6 vertices. A proof has to build a tree that branches.
- **Proved: reduction to critical graphs.** In a smallest counterexample with a degree-1 vertex x, deleting
  any vertex v ≠ x that doesn't disconnect the graph must lower the radius, by exactly one.
  Otherwise the smaller graph still has x, and minimality gives the tree.
- **Observed: critical graphs are rare and sparse.** Only 11 exist with radius ≥ 3 on up to 10 vertices,
  and every one is a tree or has exactly one cycle. For trees the result is immediate.

| Candidate lemma (graphs with a degree-1 vertex, n ≤ 9) | Verdict |
|---|---|
| tree ≥ 2r (the target) | holds on all 67,394 graphs |
| an induced path of 2r − 1 vertices plus one vertex touching it exactly once | holds |
